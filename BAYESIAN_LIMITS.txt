# Bayesian Optimization Trial Limits

Yes, the Bayesian method used in this project has a limited number of trials.

**1. Maximum Trials (`n_calls`):**
The algorithm dynamically calculates the maximum number of trials based on your total parameter space, but it is capped:
-   **Minimum:** 100 trials
-   **Maximum:** 500 trials

This means even if you have millions of combinations, the Bayesian optimizer will only test at most 500 of them.

**2. Early Stopping (Patience):**
The optimization may stop *before* reaching the maximum number of trials if the results stop improving.
-   **Mechanism:** It monitors the "best score" found so far.
-   **Patience:** The number of trials to wait for an improvement before giving up.
    -   **Low:** Stops after 10% of max trials without improvement.
    -   **Medium:** Stops after 20% of max trials without improvement (Default).
    -   **High:** Stops after 40% of max trials without improvement.

**Code Reference (`wfo.py`):**
```python
n_calls = min(500, max(100, total_combinations))
# ...
patience = max(1, int(patience_factor * n_calls))
```
